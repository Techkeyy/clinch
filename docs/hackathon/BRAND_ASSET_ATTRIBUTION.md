# CLINCH stock identity asset record

CLINCH keeps company name and ticker as the primary stock identity. Featured
cards also render a local, package-backed brand reference mark from the
installed vector packages. The browser does not fetch issuer marks, favicons,
or remote logo URLs.

These are catalogued package marks, not issuer-provided or issuer-approved
assets. CLINCH does not claim affiliation, sponsorship, partnership, or
endorsement by any upstream brand owner. Trademark rights remain with the
respective brand owner. If a mark cannot be rendered, the UI falls back to the
company name, ticker, and a restrained neutral monogram for non-featured
instruments.
