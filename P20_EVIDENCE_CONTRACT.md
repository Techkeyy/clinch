# P20 evidence contract

CLINCH keeps baseline context and researched evidence separate.

- Baseline context may show the live last price, 24-hour move, basic spread, and a basic funding snapshot when those fields are available. Baseline context is orientation only.
- A fact may support the current read, caution, Why copy, or current change trigger only when its research family completed the selected Decision Hinge.
- An unchecked, skipped, unsupported, or unavailable family must not appear as support, caution, Why, or a current trigger.
- Actual kernel skip records may appear under What CLINCH skipped. They remain truthful records of the deterministic path, not evidence.
- What would change the current read is limited to conditions grounded in completed Hinge families.
- Optional conditions from skipped families are shown separately under When to re-check. They are future monitoring conditions, not evidence for the present read.
- A stopped brief uses Remaining uncertainty for residual uncertainty after the Hinge is answered. An unresolved brief keeps the unanswered decision question open.

This contract is intentionally conservative. It does not prevent a supported instrument from being researched when a long-tail issuer has no catalogued package-backed reference mark, and it does not infer a positioning or dislocation conclusion from a baseline snapshot alone.
