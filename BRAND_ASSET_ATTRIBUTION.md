# CLINCH stock identity asset record

CLINCH uses local, build-time package-backed reference marks for stock identity surfaces. The application does not fetch issuer logos from the browser, the Bitget API, Google Images, or an unverified third-party URL. Every card also renders the company name and ticker as the authoritative text identity, and fixed mark containers prevent layout shift.

These are catalogued reference marks, not claims that CLINCH has an issuer trademark licence, partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement. Formal marketing, co-branding, press, or other uses that require permission must use the issuer's current approval process or a text-only fallback.
