# CLINCH brand-mark attribution

CLINCH uses local, build-time brand-mark data for stock identity surfaces. The application does not fetch issuer logos from the browser, the Bitget API, Google Images, or an unverified third-party URL.

## Simple Icons

The following package-backed marks are imported from `simple-icons@16.31.0`:

`AMD`, `Apple`, `Broadcom`, `Coinbase`, `Google/Alphabet`, `Intel`, `Meta`, `Netflix`, `NVIDIA`, `Palantir`, `Qualcomm`, `Shopify`, `Tesla`, and `Visa`.

Simple Icons repository: <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons>

Simple Icons license: CC0-1.0, subject to the project's per-icon source and legal disclaimer: <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md>

## Font Awesome Free Brands

The following package-backed marks are imported from `@fortawesome/free-brands-svg-icons@7.3.0`:

`Amazon` and `Microsoft`.

Font Awesome repository: <https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome>

Font Awesome Free license: icons CC BY 4.0, supporting code MIT, as documented by the upstream project: <https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/blob/6.x/LICENSE.txt>
